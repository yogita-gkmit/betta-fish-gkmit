      At Cloudera, we empower global enterprises to harness data to tackle today’s toughest challenges. We help you transform any data, anywhere, into valuable and reliable insights. Our scalable, open, and secure data management solutions deliver portable, cloud-native analytics. We enable industry leaders to turn complex data into clear, actionable insights. With our hybrid data platform, organizations can build a data-driven future by ensuring data, wherever it resides, reaches those who need it most.

      Our values aim to Empower Clouderans with a People First culture that fosters innovation, collaboration, and drives results for our business and customers, all while pursuing equality, commitment, integrity, and risk-taking.

      We attract top talent with our inclusive, collaborative culture and ensure a positive candidate experience from the start. Our employees enjoy the freedom to innovate, a healthy work-life balance, and competitive compensation. We value contributions, encourage professional growth, and enable our team to make a difference. Join us to be part of a team where your skills are recognized, your ideas matter, and your career can thrive.
      Mission: Our mission is to make data and analytics easy and accessible, for everyone. We enable global enterprises to use data to solve the impossible today.
